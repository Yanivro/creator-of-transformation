Who this is for

You don't need to become a breathwork teacher. You need breath in your toolkit.

This training is for people who already work with others, or want to, and would like to add breathwork safely and with confidence. No previous professional breathwork experience is necessary. A genuine interest in learning and exploring the breath is what matters.

The training

Breathe better. Understand your nervous system. Learn to guide others.

Discover the transformative power of the breath in this immersive 50-hour training in breathwork and advanced pranayama. It combines ancient yogic breathing techniques with modern breathwork approaches and an understanding of the nervous system, giving you a comprehensive foundation for working with the breath, both personally and professionally.

You will explore how breathing patterns influence your physical health, emotional wellbeing, energy levels and nervous system, and learn practical tools to improve your breathing, develop greater self-awareness and support regulation.

With a strong emphasis on trauma awareness, nervous system regulation and responsible facilitation, you will learn not only different breathing techniques but also when, why and how to use them.

What you'll learn

Seven modules, fifty hours.

01

Breath Fundamentals

  • Understanding the respiratory system
  • Anatomy and physiology of breathing
  • The diaphragm and breathing mechanics
  • Nasal vs. mouth breathing
  • CO₂ tolerance and oxygen utilization
  • How to improve breath capacity
02

Traditional Pranayama & Yogic Breathwork

  • Philosophy and foundations of pranayama
  • Advanced techniques
  • Bandhas and their relationship with breath
  • Integrating pranayama into your practice
03

Modern Breathwork & Functional Breathing

  • Principles of functional breathing
  • Energizing vs. calming breathwork
  • Breath awareness and breath retraining
  • Exploring different breathwork methods
  • Practical applications for everyday life
04

Breathwork & Nervous System Regulation

  • Understanding the autonomic nervous system
  • Sympathetic and parasympathetic responses
  • The role of the vagus nerve
  • Breath and stress regulation
  • The relationship between breath and emotions
  • Techniques for grounding, calming and energizing
  • Building nervous system awareness and resilience
05

Trauma Awareness & Somatic Practices

  • Understanding trauma-informed approaches
  • The relationship between breath, body and emotions
  • Recognizing signs of overwhelm and dysregulation
  • Creating safe and supportive spaces
  • Choice, consent and personal boundaries
  • Grounding and somatic awareness
  • Contraindications and responsible practice
06

Breathwork Facilitation & Integration

  • How to structure and integrate breathwork
  • Verbal cues and effective communication
  • Sequencing breathing techniques
  • Holding space for individual experiences
  • Adapting practices for different needs
  • Integrating breathwork into your offerings
  • Breathwork for therapists and coaches
07

Integration, Practice & Teaching Lab

  • Guided personal breathwork experiences
  • Daily pranayama practice
  • Teaching practice and feedback
  • Creating your own breathwork sequences
  • Integrating the learning into daily life

What you'll take away

By the end of the week, you will:

  • Understand the fundamentals of breathing anatomy and physiology.
  • Recognize and work with common breathing patterns.
  • Have a repertoire of traditional pranayama techniques.
  • Understand modern breathwork methods and their applications.
  • Know how breathing influences the nervous system.
  • Have practical tools for stress management and self-regulation.
  • Understand trauma-aware principles and responsible facilitation.
  • Be able to design and guide fundamental breathwork practices.
  • Know how to integrate breathwork into your existing profession or daily routine.

You leave with the KENAZ 50-hour certificate.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need breathwork or yoga experience?

No. The training starts from the fundamentals and builds from there. If you already teach yoga or work with clients, you'll go further with what you know.

Will I be able to guide others afterwards?

Yes, within a clear scope. You'll be able to design and guide fundamental breathwork and pranayama practices and bring them into your sessions, classes or workplace. You'll also learn the contraindications and when not to use a technique.

Is this a full breathwork facilitator training?

No. This is a 50-hour foundation in functional breathwork and pranayama. You will experience transformative breathwork yourself, but learning to facilitate deep breathwork journeys is the work of the KENAZ 200-hour training. Many people start here.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You receive the KENAZ 50-hour certificate when you complete the training.

What language is it taught in?

English.

When does it start and end?

We welcome you on Monday 11 January 2027 and finish on the morning of Monday 18 January, when you receive your certificate.

Where is it, and how do I get there?

La Tierra is a private sanctuary in the hills, about an hour from Chiang Mai, Thailand. You get yourself to Chiang Mai, and we'll send the travel details when you register.

Can I stay somewhere else?

Yes. Choose the Course option and arrange your own stay nearby. We can point you to places close to La Tierra.

What's the food like?

Every meal is chef-prepared from fresh, locally sourced ingredients. La Tierra is alcohol-free and smoke-free throughout.
