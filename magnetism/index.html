Sound familiar?

The loop.

  1. I know I'm meant to help people transform.
  2. But my sessions, workshops and retreats aren't filling, and making a living from my purpose feels further away.
  3. So I make another video. Another post. More content.
  4. It barely reaches anyone, and it drains me even more.
  5. People tell me: be more consistent. Get a better marketing plan. Sell a $29 course. Get another certificate.
  6. Every step takes me further from myself, and from what I know I'm here to do.
So I push harder…and the loop starts again.

There's a reason this loop doesn't break with more effort. In 75 minutes, we'll help you find out what it is.

Saying “I'm just not magnetic” is a misunderstanding of how physics and the universe work.

Everything in the universe, including the atoms you're made of, is built to attract and bond. That ability is already in you. The secret is finding the right conditions for it to come alive, so you can thrive and live your purpose.

The 75 minutes

What if the loop isn't a marketing problem at all?

A talk and conversation with Patricia and Yaniv, with time for your questions. You'll discover:

  • Why posting more can leave your room emptier, and what to do instead
  • What a dance floor in Germany taught Yaniv about why people actually come
  • The quiet belief behind “I'm not magnetic”, and why fighting it keeps you stuck
  • Why the $29 course and the next certificate lead you away from your gift
  • What changes when you stop chasing people and start being found
